FAQ

Before you ask

What's your typical turnaround?

Most single edits are delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Monthly retainer clients get priority slots, and rush delivery can usually be arranged if you flag the deadline in your brief.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included on every edit. Anything beyond that is scoped per project so the price stays predictable.

How do I send you footage?

Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer all work. I'll share a folder link when we start, and you drop raw footage and assets into it.

How does payment work?

Typically 50% upfront to book the slot and 50% on delivery. Payment details are confirmed when we scope the project.

How far out are you booked?

I take on a limited number of projects per month to keep quality high. Send a brief and I'll confirm current availability within 24 hours.
